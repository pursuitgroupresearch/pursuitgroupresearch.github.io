Critical care research group, Toronto

In pursuit of better critical care

We are ICU clinician-investigators who run pragmatic trials, learn from large clinical databases and bring what works back to the bedside.

Work with us Explore our research

Pragmatic, Unified Research in critical care Systems: Understanding, Implementation and Trials

Many decisions at the ICU bedside still rest on limited evidence.

Which vasopressor to start, through which line, and when to stop it. Whether a steroid helps, and for whom. We answer questions like these with trials embedded in routine care, careful analyses of large ICU databases, and implementation work that brings the results back to patients.

PURSUIT brings together intensivists, anesthesiologists, methodologists and trainees at St. Michael's Hospital, Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre and Lakeridge Health, with collaborators across Canada and around the world.

Work with us

Train with PURSUIT

We take on a small number of students and trainees from across North America. Each joins a project with a clear question and a supervisor who meets with them regularly, and leads their own analyses and papers.

  • Medical and undergraduate students

    Part-time and summer projects, most often analyses of large ICU databases such as MIMIC and eICU. We look for some research experience and comfort with R, Python or statistics.

  • Graduate students

    MSc and PhD projects in clinical epidemiology, causal inference, trial methods and machine learning. Apply early so we can talk about supervision and program fit.

  • Residents, fellows and postdocs

    Research blocks, fellowship projects and postdoctoral work in trials, target trial emulation, implementation science and AI, with the goal of first-author work.

How to apply

Apply through our online form with your CV. We review applications four times a year, in {{ site.review_months }}, and usually take on one or two new trainees each round. Priority goes to applicants who already have research experience.

Apply now

Please apply through the form. We do not review applications sent by email.
